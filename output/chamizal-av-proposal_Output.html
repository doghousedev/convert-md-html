Executive Summary

Net-AV is pleased to present this proposal for the purchase and installation of data drops and AV extenders for the Gallery and Theater at Chamizal National Memorial. As a premier AV and IT integration company specializing in presentation, communication, control, and collaboration solutions, we understand the critical nature of reliable connectivity for modern presentation spaces.

Our solution addresses the Memorial's needs for extending ethernet connectivity to strategic locations within both the Theater and Gallery spaces, along with implementing high-quality AV signal extension to support 4K video presentations. Our approach prioritizes clean installation, minimal disruption to the facility's operations, and delivers a system that will serve the Memorial's needs for years to come.

Net-AV brings over 30 years of experience integrating audiovisual and IT systems with the necessary expertise in IT, networking, electronics, acoustics, and optics to deliver a reliable solution that bridges the gap between AV and IT for the Chamizal National Memorial.

Scope of Work

Project Overview

The project involves installing ethernet data drops and AV extenders in two facilities at Chamizal National Memorial - the Theater and Gallery. These installations will enable enhanced connectivity for presentations, performances, and digital displays.

Theater Installation

  1. Network Extension To establish network connectivity throughout the Theater, begin by pulling an ethernet cable from the existing projector room located at the rear of the venue. Extend this network connection across the ceiling, routing it to three strategic locations: stage right, stage left, and behind the cyclorama. At each of these locations, install connection boxes adjacent to the existing gateways, ensuring that the network extensions are properly secured, clearly labeled, and thoroughly tested to guarantee optimal functionality and reliability.

  2. AV Extension Install HDMI-compliant 4K60 AV encoders and decoders to transport high-resolution video over standard Gigabit Ethernet. Implement an AV-over-IP system that supports 4K60 4:4:4 video specifications, ensuring seamless integration with existing systems. Test all AV extensions for signal quality and performance to guarantee optimal functionality.

Gallery Installation

  1. Network Extension To improve the functionality of the Gallery, the existing internet connectivity from the interpretation office should be extended to the Gallery space. This will involve installing appropriate network drops, allowing for the connection of computers and AV equipment. It is essential to ensure that all cabling is properly concealed, secured, and labeled to maintain a clean and professional appearance while facilitating easy identification and maintenance of the network infrastructure.

  2. AV Extension To enhance the visual experience in the Gallery, compatible AV extenders should be installed to support the digital displays. It is crucial to ensure that the AV signals meet the 4K60 4:4:4 specifications, which will guarantee optimal display quality for viewers. After the installation process is complete, thorough testing of all installations must be conducted to verify signal quality and stability, ensuring a seamless and uninterrupted viewing experience for visitors to the Gallery.

General Requirements

  1. Site Preparation Before commencing work, it is essential to conduct a thorough survey and document the existing conditions of the site. This initial step ensures that all parties involved have a clear understanding of the current state of the facility. Once the existing conditions have been assessed, the next task is to identify the most suitable cable routes that will minimize the visual impact of the installation. This process involves carefully considering the aesthetics of the space and determining the best pathways for the cables to follow, ensuring they blend seamlessly with the surroundings. To ensure a smooth and efficient installation process, it is crucial to coordinate closely with the facility staff regarding access requirements and scheduling. This collaboration allows for the proper planning and execution of the work, taking into account any specific needs or constraints of the facility. By following these steps, the cable installation can be completed effectively while maintaining the integrity and appearance of the space.

  2. Installation Standards All installations will comply with industry standards, including NEC, BICSI, and AVIXA guidelines. Plenum-rated cables will be used in all areas as required by code to ensure safety and adherence to regulations. Proper cable management techniques will be implemented throughout the installation process to maintain a professional and organized appearance. All cables, connection points, and equipment will be labeled according to Memorial's standards, facilitating easy identification and maintenance.

  3. Testing and Documentation To ensure the proper functioning and usability of the newly installed network and audiovisual infrastructure, several crucial steps must be taken. First, all network drops should be thoroughly tested for connectivity, bandwidth, and reliability to guarantee optimal performance and minimize potential issues. Similarly, all AV extensions must undergo rigorous testing to verify signal quality, latency, and compatibility with standard devices, ensuring seamless integration with existing equipment. Upon completion of the installation and testing phases, comprehensive documentation should be provided. This documentation should include as-built drawings that clearly illustrate all cable routes and connection points, facilitating future maintenance and troubleshooting. Additionally, a detailed equipment inventory, including model and serial numbers, should be compiled to aid in asset management and warranty purposes. Test results for all network and AV connections should also be included in the documentation, serving as a benchmark for performance and a reference for future comparisons. Finally, user guides should be created to instruct end-users on the proper operational use of the new connectivity points, promoting efficient and effective utilization of the upgraded infrastructure.

  4. Clean Up and Restoration Upon completion of the project, it is essential to thoroughly clean and restore the work areas to their pre-project conditions. This involves removing all packaging materials and debris from the site on a daily basis, ensuring that the area remains tidy and uncluttered throughout the duration of the project. Additionally, all staging areas must be returned to their original state, leaving no trace of the project's presence. Finally, a comprehensive inspection should be conducted to guarantee that all work areas are spotless and ready for immediate use once the project has concluded.
