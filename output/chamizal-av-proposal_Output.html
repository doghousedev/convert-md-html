Executive Summary

Net-AV is pleased to present this proposal for the purchase and installation of data drops and AV extenders for the Gallery and Theater at Chamizal National Memorial. As a premier AV and IT integration company specializing in presentation, communication, control, and collaboration solutions, we understand the critical nature of reliable connectivity for modern presentation spaces.

Our solution addresses the Memorial's needs for extending ethernet connectivity to strategic locations within both the Theater and Gallery spaces, along with implementing high-quality AV signal extension to support 4K video presentations. Our approach prioritizes clean installation, minimal disruption to the facility's operations, and delivers a system that will serve the Memorial's needs for years to come.

Net-AV brings over 30 years of experience integrating audiovisual and IT systems with the necessary expertise in IT, networking, electronics, acoustics, and optics to deliver a reliable solution that bridges the gap between AV and IT for the Chamizal National Memorial.

Scope of Work

Project Overview

The project involves installing ethernet data drops and AV extenders in two facilities at Chamizal National Memorial - the Theater and Gallery. These installations will enable enhanced connectivity for presentations, performances, and digital displays.

Theater Installation

  1. Network Extension
  2. Pull ethernet cable from the existing projector room at the rear of the Theater.
  3. Extend network connectivity across the ceiling to three strategic locations:
    • Stage right
    • Stage left
    • Behind the cyclorama
  4. Install three connection boxes adjacent to existing gateways at these locations.

  5. Ensure all network extensions are properly secured, labeled, and tested.

  6. AV Extension

  7. Install HDMI-compliant 4K60 AV encoders and decoders to transport high-resolution video over standard Gigabit Ethernet.
  8. Implement AV-over-IP system to support 4K60 4:4:4 video specifications.
  9. Ensure seamless integration with existing systems.
  10. Test all AV extensions for signal quality and performance.

Gallery Installation

  1. Network Extension
  2. Extend existing internet connectivity from the interpretation office to the Gallery.
  3. Install appropriate network drops to allow for computers and AV equipment connections.

  4. Ensure all cabling is properly concealed, secured, and labeled.

  5. AV Extension

  6. Install compatible AV extenders to support digital displays in the Gallery.
  7. Ensure AV signals meet 4K60 4:4:4 specifications for optimal display quality.
  8. Test all installations for signal quality and stability.

General Requirements

  1. Site Preparation
  2. Survey and document existing conditions before beginning work.
  3. Identify optimal cable routes to minimize visual impact.

  4. Coordinate with facility staff on access requirements and scheduling.

  5. Installation Standards

  6. All installations will comply with industry standards including NEC, BICSI, and AVIXA guidelines.
  7. Use plenum-rated cables in all areas as required by code.
  8. Implement proper cable management techniques for a professional installation.

  9. Label all cables, connection points, and equipment according to Memorial's standards.

  10. Testing and Documentation

  11. Test all network drops for connectivity, bandwidth, and reliability.
  12. Test all AV extensions for signal quality, latency, and compatibility with standard devices.

  13. Provide comprehensive documentation including:

    • As-built drawings showing all cable routes and connection points
    • Equipment inventory with model and serial numbers
    • Test results for all network and AV connections
    • User guides for operational use of new connectivity points

  14. Clean Up and Restoration

  15. Remove all packaging materials and debris from the site daily.
  16. Restore all staging areas to pre-project conditions.
  17. Ensure all work areas are clean and ready for immediate use upon project completion.

Exclusions and Assumptions

  • Work will be performed during normal business hours (Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM).
  • Project will be completed between May 1, 2025, and August 31, 2025, as specified.
  • Memorial staff will provide access to all required areas.
  • Existing network infrastructure is in good working condition.
  • No asbestos or hazardous materials will be encountered during installation.
  • Electrical outlets are available and adequate for all required equipment.
  • No structural modifications are required for cable routing.
