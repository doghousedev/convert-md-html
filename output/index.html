Microsoft Teams Upgrade Proposal for ACC-DTA Conference Rooms

Prepared by: Net-AV
Date: April 22, 2025
Project: Conference Room Upgrade and Maintenance
Reference: RFI #528356

1. Executive Summary

Net-AV is pleased to present this comprehensive proposal for upgrading the video teleconferencing (VTC) capabilities in three conference rooms at the Army Contracting Command-Detroit Arsenal (ACC-DTA). As requested in RFI #528356, our solution will replace the existing Cisco Codec VTC systems and cameras with Microsoft Teams-compatible solutions while maintaining full functionality of the existing audio-visual infrastructure.

As a woman-owned small business with extensive experience in AV integration for government facilities, Net-AV is uniquely positioned to deliver this project efficiently and effectively. Our team of certified AV professionals has specific experience with Crestron, Biamp, and Microsoft Teams integration, ensuring a seamless transition from Cisco-based VTC to Microsoft Teams.

2. Project Understanding

The ACC-DTA seeks to upgrade three spaces: The Executive Director's Office is located next to the Executive Conference Room on the same floor. Downstairs, in the basement, there is a Training Room available for various meetings and workshops. The office of the Executive Director overlooks the Executive Conference Room, making it convenient for meetings and discussions to take place. The Training Room in the basement provides a private and secluded area for employees to engage in training sessions and workshops without interruptions. These three spaces are essential for the smooth operation and communication within the organization.

Each space currently uses Cisco SX80 VTC systems with Precision 60 cameras that will be phased out with the upcoming NIPR VTC sunset. This project requires:

  1. Removal of existing Cisco VTC codecs and cameras
  2. Installation of new Microsoft Teams-compatible cameras
  3. Reprogramming of touch panels to remove VTC functionality and integrate with Teams
  4. Updating user interfaces for simplified operation
  5. Ensuring all existing equipment remains functional
  6. Providing training, documentation, and warranty support

3. System Design Approach

Our design approach focuses on maximizing the reuse of existing infrastructure while ensuring full compatibility with Microsoft Teams. The key components of our solution include:

3.1. Microsoft Teams Room (MTR) Integration

Each room will be equipped with a Microsoft Teams Room system comprising:

The Teams-certified cameras are high-quality 1080p/60fps PTZ cameras that have been optimized specifically for Teams meetings. These cameras ensure a clear and crisp image during video conferences. The compute system is comprised of Microsoft Teams Room-certified computer systems that have the appropriate processing power to handle the demands of Teams meetings. This ensures smooth and uninterrupted communication. Additionally, there is full integration with existing infrastructure such as Crestron control systems, Biamp audio processors, and display technologies. This compatibility allows for seamless operation and enhances the overall meeting experience.

The existing Crestron touch panels will be reprogrammed to:

Executive Conference Room

Technical Implementation Details

Microsoft Teams Room Upgrade for ACC-DTA

Equipment Specifications

Microsoft Teams Room Systems

We recommend implementing Microsoft-certified Teams Room systems for each location. For this project, we propose using the following configuration:

  1. Compute Platform: Lenovo ThinkSmart Core or equivalent To ensure optimal performance, the system should be equipped with an Intel Core i5 processor or better, at least 8GB of RAM, and a minimum of 128GB SSD storage. It should also run on Windows 10 IoT Enterprise and have the Microsoft Teams Rooms app installed. These specifications will provide a seamless and efficient experience for users.

  2. Cameras: In the Executive Director's Office, we will be using the Logitech Rally Camera or an equivalent model. This camera is equipped with 1080p/60fps capability, ensuring high-quality video output. It also features a 15x optical zoom and a wide 90° field of view, allowing for clear and comprehensive coverage of the room. The camera has pan/tilt/zoom functionality for flexibility in adjusting the view. With USB 3.0 connectivity, it can easily connect to various devices for seamless integration. Additionally, the camera is remote control capable, providing convenience and ease of use.

In the executive conference room, we will be utilizing the Logitech Rally Camera or an equivalent model. This camera is equipped with 1080p/60fps capability, 15x optical zoom, and a 90° field of view. It also offers pan/tilt/zoom functionality for optimal control during video conferences. Additionally, the camera can be easily connected via USB 3.0 for seamless integration into our existing technology setup.

The basement training room is equipped with three PTZ Optics 12X-SDI-GY-G2 cameras, which offer 1080p/60fps capability, a 12x optical zoom, and a 72.5° field of view. These cameras also feature pan/tilt/zoom functionality and are equipped with SDI and USB 3.0 outputs.

  1. Auxiliary Equipment: USB extenders are available as needed for any additional length required. HDMI-to-USB capture devices can be provided if necessary. Control system integration modules are also available for seamless integration. Cable management solutions are offered to keep cords organized and tidy.

System Integration Details

1. Executive Director's Office

Current Equipment to Remain: The office setup includes a Dell WD19 Docking Station, two Cisco Table Microphones, a display monitor, and a Crestron touch panel.

Integration Approach: 1. Remove Cisco SX80 codec and Precision 60 camera 2. Install Microsoft Teams Room compute system 3. Install new PTZ camera on wall mount (same location as existing) 4. Integrate with existing microphones via USB audio interface 5. Reprogram touch panel for Teams control 6. Configure system for one-touch meeting join 7. Program room control for lighting and display management

Signal Flow: The Camera is connected to the Teams Room System via USB, while the Microphones are connected to the Teams Room System through a USB Audio Interface. The Teams Room System then connects to the Display using an HDMI cable. Additionally, the Control System communicates with the Teams Room System through the network.

2. Executive Conference Room

Current Equipment to Remain: The setup includes four Sharp TVs (2x PN-LE901, 2x LE-701), a Crestron DM MD8X8 Digital Media Switcher, a Crestron DM-RMC-4K-SCALER-C RECEIVER, a Crestron DM-TX-4K-302-C DigitalMedia Transmitter, a Crestron CP3N, a BIAMP TESIRAFORTE AVB VT, a Crestron AMP-2210HT power amplifier, six ceiling speakers (Crestron SAROS_IC6T), three beamtracking ceiling microphones (Biamp Tesira TCM-1EX), and a Crestron DM-MD-8X8 video matrix.

Integration Approach: 1. Remove Cisco SX80 codec and Precision 60 camera 2. Install Microsoft Teams Room compute system 3. Install new PTZ camera on wall mount (same location as existing) 4. Integrate with Biamp audio system via USB audio connection 5. Reprogram Crestron control system for display management 6. Configure system for content sharing to multiple displays 7. Update touch panel interface for intuitive operation

Signal Flow: The camera, Biamp audio system, and Teams Room System are all connected via USB in the conferencing setup. The Teams Room System is then connected to the Crestron DM Matrix through HDMI, which in turn connects to the displays. Content sharing devices also connect to the Crestron DM Matrix via HDMI. Lastly, the control system communicates with the Teams Room System through the network.

3. Basement Training Room

Current Equipment to Remain: The setup includes a total of five displays, consisting of one NEC 75" LCD TV V754Q and four NEC 98" V984Q screens. In addition, there are eleven ELECTROVOICE C6.2 in-ceiling speakers for audio output and six AUDIX M3W ceiling microphones for capturing sound. To control the system, a Crestron touchpanel TS-1070-W-S is also included.

Integration Approach: 1. Remove Cisco SX80 codec 2. Replace three PTZ Optics cameras with Teams-compatible equivalents 3. Install Microsoft Teams Room compute system 4. Integrate with existing audio system 5. Configure camera switching for Teams meetings 6. Reprogram touch panel for camera selection and Teams control 7. Set up content routing to all displays

Signal Flow: The three PTZ cameras connect to the Teams Room System via USB, as does the audio system. The Teams Room System then connects to the Video Distribution System via HDMI, which in turn displays the content on various displays. The control system communicates with the Teams Room System, cameras, and audio system through the network.

Maintenance Recommendations

Routine Maintenance Tasks:

  1. Monthly (User Performed): Before beginning any work, it is important to dust and clean all visible equipment to ensure proper functionality. Additionally, it is crucial to verify that the cameras are positioned correctly for optimal performance. Finally, it is recommended to check for any loose connections that could potentially disrupt the system. By following these steps, you can help maintain the efficiency and effectiveness of the equipment.

  2. Quarterly (Technician Performed): Before using the cameras, it is important to clean the lenses to ensure clear images. Next, all system functions should be tested to make sure they are working properly. It is also essential to check for any firmware updates to keep the cameras up to date. Additionally, verifying network connectivity is crucial for remote access and monitoring. Finally, if applicable, testing backup systems is necessary to ensure that footage is properly saved in case of any technical issues.

  3. Biannual (Contractor Performed): During the maintenance process, various tasks are completed to ensure the system is functioning properly. This includes comprehensive system testing to identify any issues or malfunctions. Firmware and software updates are also performed to keep the system up to date with the latest technology. Cable inspection and testing are conducted to check for any damage or faults that may affect the system's performance. Additionally, an audio system calibration check is done to ensure optimal sound quality. Control system programming updates are made as needed to enhance the system's functionality and efficiency.

Preventive Maintenance Checklist:

During routine maintenance, it is important to inspect camera mounts to ensure stability and clean air filters in equipment. Additionally, signal levels should be measured and recorded, and battery backup systems should be tested if applicable. It is also crucial to verify the responsiveness of the control system and test all source inputs and outputs. Display color accuracy should be measured, and the audio system should be checked for noise or distortion. These steps help ensure that all equipment is functioning properly and will provide high-quality results.
