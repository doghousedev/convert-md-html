Executive Summary

Net-AV is pleased to present this proposal for the purchase and installation of data drops and AV extenders for the Gallery and Theater at Chamizal National Memorial. As a premier AV and IT integration company specializing in presentation, communication, control, and collaboration solutions, we understand the critical nature of reliable connectivity for modern presentation spaces.

Our solution addresses the Memorial's needs for extending ethernet connectivity to strategic locations within both the Theater and Gallery spaces, along with implementing high-quality AV signal extension to support 4K video presentations. Our approach prioritizes clean installation, minimal disruption to the facility's operations, and delivers a system that will serve the Memorial's needs for years to come.

Net-AV brings over 30 years of experience integrating audiovisual and IT systems with the necessary expertise in IT, networking, electronics, acoustics, and optics to deliver a reliable solution that bridges the gap between AV and IT for the Chamizal National Memorial.

Scope of Work

Project Overview

The project involves installing ethernet data drops and AV extenders in two facilities at Chamizal National Memorial - the Theater and Gallery. These installations will enable enhanced connectivity for presentations, performances, and digital displays.

Theater Installation

  1. Network Extension In order to improve network connectivity in the Theater, the ethernet cable in the existing projector room at the rear needs to be pulled. This cable will then be extended across the ceiling to three strategic locations: stage right, stage left, and behind the cyclorama. At each of these locations, a connection box will be installed next to the existing gateways. It is important to ensure that all network extensions are properly secured, labeled, and tested to guarantee optimal performance.

  2. AV Extension To transport high-resolution video over standard Gigabit Ethernet, HDMI-compliant 4K60 AV encoders and decoders will be installed. Additionally, an AV-over-IP system will be implemented to support 4K60 4:4:4 video specifications. It is crucial to ensure seamless integration with existing systems. To guarantee signal quality and performance, all AV extensions will be thoroughly tested.

Gallery Installation

  1. Network Extension To improve internet connectivity in the Gallery, the existing internet connection from the interpretation office will be extended. This will involve installing network drops to enable computers and AV equipment to connect to the network. Additionally, all cabling will be concealed, secured, and labeled to ensure a tidy and organized setup.

  2. AV Extension To enhance the digital displays in the Gallery, it is essential to install compatible AV extenders. These extenders should support AV signals that meet 4K60 4:4:4 specifications, ensuring optimal display quality. Before finalizing the installations, it is crucial to test all setups for signal quality and stability. This thorough testing process will help guarantee that the digital displays function effectively and provide a high-quality viewing experience for visitors.

General Requirements

  1. Site Preparation Before beginning any work, it is essential to conduct a thorough survey and document the existing conditions. This will provide a clear understanding of the environment and any potential obstacles that may need to be addressed. In order to minimize visual impact, it is important to identify optimal cable routes. By carefully planning the placement of cables, the overall aesthetic of the area can be preserved. Additionally, coordination with facility staff is crucial to ensure that access requirements are met and that scheduling conflicts are avoided. Working closely with facility staff will help to streamline the installation process and minimize disruptions.

  2. Installation Standards All installations at Memorial will adhere to industry standards such as NEC, BICSI, and AVIXA guidelines. Plenum-rated cables must be used in all areas in order to meet code requirements. Proper cable management techniques will be implemented to ensure a professional installation. Additionally, all cables, connection points, and equipment will be labeled according to Memorial's standards.

  3. Testing and Documentation During the implementation process, it is crucial to test all network drops for connectivity, bandwidth, and reliability. Additionally, it is important to test all AV extensions for signal quality, latency, and compatibility with standard devices. To ensure a seamless transition, comprehensive documentation should be provided, including as-built drawings showing all cable routes and connection points, an equipment inventory with model and serial numbers, test results for all network and AV connections, and user guides for operational use of new connectivity points. This documentation will not only streamline the implementation process but also serve as a valuable resource for future maintenance and troubleshooting.

  4. Clean Up and Restoration Throughout the duration of the project, it is essential to remove all packaging materials and debris from the site on a daily basis. Additionally, staging areas must be restored to their pre-project conditions to ensure efficiency and organization. Lastly, before project completion, all work areas should be cleaned thoroughly and made ready for immediate use. These steps will help maintain a clean and safe work environment throughout the project.

Exclusions and Assumptions

The work for this project will be carried out during normal business hours, specifically Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM. The project is scheduled to be completed between May 1, 2025, and August 31, 2025, as outlined. The Memorial staff will grant access to all necessary areas for the project. The current network infrastructure is in good working order, and no asbestos or hazardous materials are expected to be encountered during the installation process. There are sufficient electrical outlets available to accommodate all required equipment. Additionally, no structural changes are needed for cable routing purposes.
