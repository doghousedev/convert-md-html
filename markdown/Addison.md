## Executive Summary

Net-AV is pleased to present this AV integration proposal for the WT-Addison new construction multi-dwelling project. Based on our understanding of your requirements, we will provide and install display systems for the fitness areas and common spaces across two buildings. 

This proposal outlines our comprehensive solution, including commercial-grade displays, professional mounting hardware, and expert installation services that will enhance the resident experience in your new development. Our installation will ensure that all displays are securely mounted, properly connected, and ready for use upon completion.

The scope includes installation of 55" displays in fitness areas and larger 75" displays in the lobby and club room, along with all necessary mounting hardware, cabling, and connections. Net-AV will manage all aspects of the project from design through installation and testing, providing turnkey service with minimal disruption to your construction schedule.

## Comprehensive System Overview

The proposed AV solution for WT-Addison consists of four commercial-grade display installations across two buildings. Each display is strategically positioned to maximize visibility and enhance the user experience in common areas. The solution includes:

- Professional-grade commercial displays selected for durability and continuous operation
- Heavy-duty wall mounting solutions suitable for commercial environments
- Professional installation including mounting, cabling, and connections
- Project management to coordinate with construction timelines
- Testing and commissioning of all systems

Each installation will include backboxes for clean cable management and power, professional-grade wall mounts rated for the specified display size, and cable management solutions to ensure a clean, finished appearance. All installations will comply with building codes and industry best practices.

## Implementation and Project Management

Net-AV will provide comprehensive project management to ensure seamless integration of the AV systems into your construction timeline. Our implementation process includes:

1. **Design and Engineering (2 weeks):**
   - Finalize equipment selections
   - Coordinate with construction team on backbox locations
   - Prepare detailed installation drawings

2. **Equipment Procurement (3-4 weeks):**
   - Order all equipment
   - Stage and test equipment at Net-AV facility
   - Coordinate delivery with construction schedule

3. **Installation (1-2 weeks, coordinated with construction):**
   - Install backboxes during rough-in phase
   - Mount displays and connect cabling
   - Test and commission systems

4. **Project Management:**
   - Dedicated project manager to coordinate with construction team
   - Regular status updates
   - Quality assurance inspections
   - Final walkthrough and client training

5. **Closeout:**
   - System testing and verification
   - Client training and documentation
   - Warranty and service information

## Standard Inclusions and Exclusions

### Inclusions:

- Access for Net-AV integration and engineering staff to the rooms during normal business hours for installation. Exact scheduling to be coordinated with Clear Connection staff prior to installation. At Net-AV request, off hours work will be granted to support the installation needs.

- Access to building facility engineer and or other staff, as needed, during design phase and installation for coordination of cable paths and access locations.

- Clear Connection will provide a working network connection for the video conferencing system.

- Clear Connection will provide Net-AV any IP addresses or other relevant network settings and/or information to allow set up of the audiovisual system components. Net-AV will work with the Clear Connection IT staff to acquire this information.

- It will be the Clear Connection's responsibility to manage the network and network attached devices.

- Clear Connection will supply any laptop, tablets or computer(s) for the system.

- Clear Connection will provide for any electrical work required within the room and is responsible for any relevant permits for such electrical work.

- Clear Connection will specifically supply any AC power connections required at the equipment racks or credenzas. Some work may require electrical service (not supplied under this proposal) if standard AC outlet not available in proximity to the racks or credenzas.

- Clear Connection will supply G/OFE equipment to Net-AV for integration in proper working order, and in a timely manner, so as not to impact the delivery schedule.

- Where applicable, the owner's architect will provide Net-AV's engineering department with all required architectural floor, reflected ceiling, building elevation, and section plans in Auto Cad format at no charge to Net-AV.

- Clear Connection will supply the appropriate software keys for multi-point calls and/or external bridging for multi-point calls.

- Clear Connection will supply required IP addresses and accessibility if the system is to part of the network.

- Software licensing -- Where applicable Net-AV will provide access to source code written specifically for the operation of the control systems. This will be provided under a licensing agreement limiting the duplication and distribution of software written by Net-AV for the specific use within the systems installed by Net-AV.

### Exclusions:

- All conduits, high voltage wiring panels, breakers, relays, boxes, receptacles, etc. Any related electrical work, including but not limited to 110VAC, conduit, core drilling, raceway and boxes.

- Voice/data cabling, POTS/ analog phone lines, ISDN lines, network ports, etc.

- Network connectivity, routing, switching and port configuration necessary to support audio-visual equipment.

- Concrete saw cutting and/or core drilling.

- Fire wall, ceiling, roof and floor penetration, patching, removal or fire stopping.

- Necessary ceiling tile or T-bar modifications, replacement and/or repair.

- Any and all millwork (moldings, trim, etc.) and/or furniture. All millwork and/or furniture or modifications to project millwork to accommodate the AV equipment is to be provided by others unless otherwise noted in this proposal.

- Painting, patching or finishing of architectural surfaces.

- Government/Owner furnished equipment (G/OFE) or equipment by others Installation of devices, systems, hardware, software as outlined in the statement of work.

- Permits (unless specifically provided for elsewhere in the contract).

- HVAC and plumbing relocation.

- Rough-in, bracing, framing, or finish trim carpentry for installation. Structural support unless otherwise specified

- Cutting, structural welding, or reinforcement of structural steel members required for support of assemblies, as required.

- Any applicable taxes, permits or bonds related to the project.

- Government/Owner furnished equipment (G/OFE) or equipment by others that is integrated into the systems (as described above) is assumed to be current industry acceptable equipment in good working order. If it is determined that this equipment is faulty upon installation or adversely affects the system additional charges may be incurred.
