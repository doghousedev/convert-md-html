## Statement of Work

### Large Conference Room

#### General Overview
The Large Conference Room will be equipped with a comprehensive audio-visual system designed to support presentations, video conferencing, and media playback. The system will provide high-quality audio reproduction and clear visual displays suitable for various meeting types and audience sizes.

#### Display Systems
- One (1) Samsung QE85T 85" Class 4K UHD Commercial LED Display
- One (1) Chief XTM1U Micro-Adjust Tilt Wall Mount for secure display installation
- One (1) Extron DTP2 R 211 HDMI 4K/60 DTP2 Receiver with Audio De-Embedding

#### Audio Systems
- Six (6) Extron SoundField XD Low Profile 6.5" Two-Way Ceiling Speakers
- One (1) Biamp TesiraFORTE AVB VT4 DSP for audio processing
- One (1) Biamp Parlé TCM-1 ceiling microphone (white)
- Two (2) Biamp Parlé TCM-1EX ceiling microphone extensions (white)
- One (1) Extron XPA 2001-70V One Channel Amp, 200 watts at 70 volts

#### Control Systems
- One (1) Extron 7" Tabletop TouchLink Pro Touchpanel (black)
- One (1) Extron 7" Wall Mount TouchLink Scheduling Panel (black)
- One (1) Extron DTP3 IN2004 DI/DO Switcher and Control Processor

#### Video Systems
- One (1) Vaddio RoboSHOT 12E OneLink Bridge System for video conferencing
- One (1) Extron ShareLink Pro 1100 Wired and Wireless Presentation System
- One (1) Extron WFA 100 US USB to Wi-Fi & Bluetooth Adapter
- One (1) Extron DVC RGB-HD A RGB to HDMI Converter with Audio Embedding
- One (1) Extron ConferenceShare Upgrade Link License
- One (1) Avocent SV340DPH-400 SwitchView Dual Head 4-port Desktop KVM switch

#### IT Hardware
- One (1) Netgear GSM4212P AV Line M4250-10G2F-PoE+ Managed Switch (8x1G PoE+ 125W 2x1G and 2xSFP)

#### Racks and Infrastructure
- One (1) Net-AV EV-RKIK-24 Rack installation kit including blanks, mounting brackets, equipment shelves, and hardware
- One (1) Extron Digital Signage Upgrade LinkLicense for ShareLink Pro

### PA System

#### General Overview
The PA System will provide comprehensive audio coverage throughout the facility, supporting announcements, emergency notifications, and background music as needed. The system is designed for clarity, reliability, and appropriate volume levels for all designated areas.

#### Audio Systems
- Fifty-six (56) Extron SoundField XD Low Profile 6.5" Two-Way Ceiling Speakers with 4.2" Composite Back
- Ten (10) Extron SpeedMount Two-Way Surface Mount Speakers with 6.5" Woofer and 70/100V Transformer (black)
- One (1) Extron XPA U 758 Eight Channel Amp, 75 watts at 8 ohms
- One (1) Bogen DFT120 Digital Feedback Eliminator
- One (1) Bogen UTI1 Telephone Interface Module for phone paging system (single-zone, universal)

#### Infrastructure
- All necessary cabling, connectors, and mounting hardware for complete system integration
- Zone control capability for selective paging and volume adjustments by area
- Properly sized amplification for coverage requirements

## Standard Net-AV Inclusions and Exclusions

### Inclusions

- Access for Net-AV integration and engineering staff to the room during normal business hours for installation. Exact scheduling to be coordinated with BoMark staff prior to installation. At Net-AV request, off-hours work will be granted to support the installation needs.

- Access to building facility engineer and or other staff, as needed, during design phase and installation for coordination of cable paths and access locations.

- BoMark will provide a working network connection for the video conferencing system.

- BoMark will provide Net-AV any IP addresses or other relevant network settings and/or information to allow set up of the audiovisual system components. Net-AV will work with the BoMark IT staff to acquire this information.

- It will be BoMark's responsibility to manage the network and network attached devices.

- BoMark will supply any laptop, tablets or computer(s) for the system.

- BoMark will provide for any electrical work required within the room and is responsible for any relevant permits for such electrical work.

- BoMark will specifically supply any AC power connections required at the equipment racks or credenzas. Some work may require electrical service (not supplied under this proposal) if standard AC outlet not available in proximity to the racks or credenzas.

- BoMark will supply G/OFE equipment to Net-AV for integration in proper working order, and in a timely manner, so as not to impact the delivery schedule.

- Where applicable, the owner's architect will provide Net-AV's engineering department with all required architectural floor, reflected ceiling, building elevation, and section plans in Auto Cad format at no charge to Net-AV.

- BoMark will supply the appropriate software keys for multi-point calls and/or external bridging for multi-point calls.

- BoMark will supply required IP addresses and accessibility if the system is to part of the network.

- Software licensing -- Where applicable Net-AV will provide access to source code written specifically for the operation of the control systems. This will be provided under a licensing agreement limiting the duplication and distribution of software written by Net-AV for the specific use within the systems installed by Net-AV.

### Exclusions

- All conduits, high voltage wiring panels, breakers, relays, boxes, receptacles, etc. Any related electrical work, including but not limited to 110VAC, conduit, core drilling, raceway and boxes.

- Voice/data cabling, POTS/ analog phone lines, ISDN lines, network ports, etc.

- Network connectivity, routing, switching and port configuration necessary to support audio-visual equipment.

- Concrete saw cutting and/or core drilling.

- Fire wall, ceiling, roof and floor penetration, patching, removal or fire stopping.

- Necessary ceiling tile or T-bar modifications, replacement and/or repair.

- Any and all millwork (moldings, trim, etc.) and/or furniture. All millwork and/or furniture or modifications to project millwork to accommodate the AV equipment is to be provided by others unless otherwise noted in this proposal.

- Painting, patching or finishing of architectural surfaces.

- Government/Owner furnished equipment (G/OFE) or equipment by others Installation of devices, systems, hardware, software as outlined in the statement of work.

- Permits (unless specifically provided for elsewhere in the contract).

- HVAC and plumbing relocation.

- Rough-in, bracing, framing, or finish trim carpentry for installation. Structural support unless otherwise specified

- Cutting, structural welding, or reinforcement of structural steel members required for support of assemblies, as required.

- Any applicable taxes, permits or bonds related to the project.

- Government/Owner furnished equipment (G/OFE) or equipment by others that is integrated into the systems (as described above) is assumed to be current industry acceptable equipment in good working order. If it is determined that this equipment is faulty upon installation or adversely affects the system additional charges may be incurred.
