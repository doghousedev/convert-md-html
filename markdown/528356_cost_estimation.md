### Budget Notes

1. **Equipment Selection**
   - All equipment is commercial-grade and Microsoft Teams certified
   - Equipment costs include standard manufacturer warranty
   - Where possible, we've specified equipment compatible with existing infrastructure
   - Pricing based on current market rates (April 2025)

2. **Labor Estimates**
   - Labor rates align with industry standards for specialized AV integration
   - Hours estimated based on similar conference room upgrade projects
   - Installation hours allow for careful removal of existing equipment
   - Programming includes development, testing, and on-site adjustments

3. **Travel Costs**
   - Based on travel from Net-AV's Westminster, MD location to Detroit Arsenal
   - Includes airfare, lodging, local transportation, and per diem
   - Site survey trip required to verify existing conditions
   - Installation trips include mobilization and equipment installation
   - Final trips for commissioning and training

4. **Contingency**
   - 10% contingency included to account for unforeseen conditions
   - Covers potential equipment substitutions if specified items become unavailable
   - Addresses possible additional programming requirements
   - Provides buffer for any site-specific challenges discovered during installation

5. **Exclusions**
   - Network infrastructure upgrades or modifications
   - Structural modifications to walls, ceilings, or floors
   - Electrical work beyond standard equipment connections
   - Furniture modifications or replacements
   - Government-furnished equipment

6. **Optional Items (Not Included in Total)**
   - Extended warranty beyond 1 year: $7,500/year
   - Additional training sessions: $1,500/day
   - Remote support package: $4,800/year
   - After-hours installation (to minimize disruption): $5,200
   - Preventive maintenance visits (quarterly): $6,400/year

### Return on Investment Considerations

1. **Operational Efficiency**
   - Reduces meeting setup time by approximately 5-7 minutes per meeting
   - Improves collaboration with standardized Microsoft Teams platform
   - Simplifies scheduling and joining meetings
   - Reduces IT support requirements for troubleshooting

2. **Infrastructure Value**
   - Leverages existing AV infrastructure (est. $150,000+ value)
   - Provides upgrade path for future capabilities without full replacement
   - Standardizes on Microsoft Teams platform used throughout organization
   - Extends useful life of existing equipment by 5+ years

3. **Training & Adoption Benefits**
   - Familiar Microsoft Teams interface reduces learning curve
   - Consistent experience across multiple spaces
   - Reduced user frustration and support calls
   - Improved meeting attendance and participation

4. **Total Cost of Ownership**
   - Eliminates Cisco licensing costs (approximately $4,500/year)
   - Reduces maintenance costs by approximately 25%
   - Standardizes on one platform, reducing support complexity
   - Provides easier path for future upgrades
