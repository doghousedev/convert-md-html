# Statement of Work

## Overview

Net-AV is pleased to submit this proposal for the Bomark BSO Phase 2 project. This proposal outlines the audiovisual systems for the Musician's Lounge and Green Room spaces within the Baltimore Symphony Orchestra facility, as detailed in the architectural drawings provided. Our comprehensive solution includes equipment, installation, programming, and testing designed to meet the specific needs of each space.

## Basement Musician's Lounge

### General Overview

The Musician's Lounge will be equipped with a versatile audiovisual system designed for casual viewing, information sharing, and relaxation. The system includes multiple displays, wireless content sharing capabilities, and distributed audio to create a comfortable environment for BSO musicians. All installation will be performed in accordance with the architectural drawings and installation details provided.

### Display Systems

The Musician's Lounge will feature two professional-grade displays:

- One (1) LG 86" UH5J-H large format display for primary viewing mounted on the main wall using Chief mounting hardware with appropriate back box as shown in detail #5 on drawing sheet
- One (1) LG 55" UM340E0UZ medium format display for secondary viewing located in the dining area with appropriate mounting hardware as detailed in drawing #3

Both displays will be professionally mounted using Chief mounting solutions with appropriate back boxes for clean installation. The 86" display will utilize a TS525TU mount with a TAS1 1-inch spacer kit and TA500 back box, while the 55" display will be installed with an appropriately sized mount.

### Video Systems

The video system will include:

- One (1) Contemporary Research 232-ATSC 4K HDTV tuner providing cable television content
- One (1) Crestron AM-3100-WF wireless content sharing receiver allowing users to easily present content from personal devices
- HDMI connectivity for local source connection

### Audio Systems

The audio system will consist of:

- Three (3) pairs of Atlas GD87W ceiling speakers (six speakers total) strategically located for even coverage throughout the space as shown in the reflected ceiling plan
- Two (2) Atlas AT35-PA volume controls for independent audio level adjustment in different zones
- Audio distribution tied to video sources with ability to select between sources

### Cabling & Infrastructure

All cabling will be installed in accordance with the specifications in the technical details and general notes provided in the architectural drawings. This includes:

- Plenum-rated cables in accessible ceiling spaces
- Appropriate cable support systems per specifications
- Cable labeling as per industry standards
- Conduit and accessories as detailed in the telecommunications details

## Green Room

### General Overview

The Green Room will be equipped with a comprehensive audiovisual system designed for presentations, video conferencing, and entertainment as detailed in the drawings. This system provides greater functionality than the Musician's Lounge, featuring integrated control, video conferencing capabilities, and professional audio, all installed according to the detailed architectural drawings.

### Display Systems

The Green Room will feature multiple displays:

- One (1) LG 86" UH5J-H large format display as the primary viewing screen, mounted according to detail #3 in the drawings
- Two (2) LG 32" SM5J-B small format displays for auxiliary viewing and confidence monitors
- Installation using specified mounting hardware to ensure safety and aesthetic integrity

### Video Systems

The Green Room video system includes:

- One (1) Contemporary Research 232-ATSC 4K HDTV tuner
- QSC NV-series AV over IP infrastructure (NV-32-H processor and NV-21-HU decoders)
- Two (2) Crestron AM-TX3-100 wireless transmitters
- One (1) Crestron DM-NAX-BTIO-1G Bluetooth wallplate receiver for wireless audio streaming
- HDMI connectivity for local source connection
- Integration with video conferencing system
- Owner-provided Yodeck digital signage player integration

### Audio Systems

The professional audio system consists of:

- Four (4) JBL Control 26CT ceiling speakers for high-quality audio reinforcement
- Required JBL MTC-TCD dogear kits for ceiling installation
- One (1) Extron MPA 601 audio amplifier (60W, 70V) for speaker distribution
- One (1) Shure MXA920AL-R beamforming ceiling microphone for video conferencing with appropriate pendant mounting

### Control Systems

The Green Room features an integrated control system:

- One (1) QSC TSC-70-G3 7" touch panel for intuitive system control
- Required Q-SYS software licenses (Script, Stream, and UCI)
- QSC Q-SYS ecosystem providing comprehensive system management
- One (1) QSC QIO-GP8x8 GPIO I/O Control Device
- One (1) QSC QIO-S4 GPIO RS232 Controller
- Full programming of control interface as detailed in the system logic diagrams

### Video Conferencing

The video conferencing system includes:

- One (1) Logitech Rally USB PTZ camera
- One (1) Vaddio 999-1005-032 USB extender RX
- One (1) Vaddio 999-1005-032 USB extender TX
- One (1) Vaddio 999-2225-118 in-wall camera enclosure
- Integration with audio and control systems as per logical diagram specifications

### Network Infrastructure

The system is supported by robust network infrastructure:

- One (1) QSC NS26-1440++ PoE network switch for AV over IP distribution
- Structured cabling with Category 6 connectivity
- Middle Atlantic equipment rack with power distribution

## Installation Process

The installation process will follow these steps for both rooms:

1. **Site Preparation**: Confirm all power and network requirements have been met according to drawings
2. **Infrastructure Installation**: Install all mounting hardware, brackets, and back boxes
3. **Cable Installation**: Run all required cabling according to specifications and cable schedules
4. **Equipment Installation**: Mount displays, speakers, and other equipment
5. **Equipment Rack Assembly**: Build, wire, and test equipment rack
6. **System Connection**: Connect all components according to system diagrams
7. **System Programming**: Program control system and configure all components
8. **Testing and Commissioning**: Thoroughly test all system functions
9. **Training**: Provide user training on system operation
10. **Documentation**: Provide as-built drawings and system manuals

## Project Management and Quality Control

Net-AV will provide dedicated project management services throughout the project lifecycle. This includes:

1. **Project Planning**: Development of detailed project schedule and resource allocation
2. **Communication**: Regular client updates and coordination with other trades
3. **Issue Management**: Prompt resolution of any issues that arise
4. **Quality Control**: Rigorous testing and validation of all systems
5. **Documentation**: Comprehensive project documentation
6. **Client Acceptance**: Formal client acceptance process

# Standard Net-AV Inclusions and Exclusions

Net-AV provides AV systems integration under the following standard inclusions and exclusions. In the event that there is a conflict between Net-AV's standard inclusions/exclusions and those set forth in drawing documents, the drawing documents shall take precedence:

## Inclusions

- Access for Net-AV integration and engineering staff to the room during normal business hours for installation. Exact scheduling to be coordinated with Bomark staff prior to installation. At Net-AV request, off hours work will be granted to support the installation needs.

- Access to building facility engineer and or other staff, as needed, during design phase and installation for coordination of cable paths and access locations.

- Bomark will provide a working network connection for the video conferencing system.

- Bomark will provide Net-AV any IP addresses or other relevant network settings and/or information to allow set up of the audiovisual system components. Net-AV will work with the Bomark IT staff to acquire this information.

- It will be the Bomark's responsibility to manage the network and network attached devices.

- Bomark will supply any laptop, tablets or computer(s) for the system.

- Bomark will provide for any electrical work required within the room and is responsible for any relevant permits for such electrical work.

- Bomark will specifically supply any AC power connections required at the equipment racks or credenzas. Some work may require electrical service (not supplied under this proposal) if standard AC outlet not available in proximity to the racks or credenzas.

- Bomark will supply G/OFE equipment to Net-AV for integration in proper working order, and in a timely manner, so as not to impact the delivery schedule.

- Where applicable, the owner's architect will provide Net-AV's engineering department with all required architectural floor, reflected ceiling, building elevation, and section plans in Auto Cad format at no charge to Net-AV.

- Bomark will supply the appropriate software keys for multi-point calls and/or external bridging for multi-point calls.

- Bomark will supply required IP addresses and accessibility if the system is to part of the network.

- Software licensing – Where applicable Net-AV will provide access to source code written specifically for the operation of the control systems. This will be provided under a licensing agreement limiting the duplication and distribution of software written by Net-AV for the specific use within the systems installed by Net-AV.

## Exclusions

- All conduits, high voltage wiring panels, breakers, relays, boxes, receptacles, etc. Any related electrical work, including but not limited to 110VAC, conduit, core drilling, raceway and boxes.

- Voice/data cabling, POTS/ analog phone lines, ISDN lines, network ports, etc.

- Network connectivity, routing, switching and port configuration necessary to support audio-visual equipment.

- Concrete saw cutting and/or core drilling.

- Fire wall, ceiling, roof and floor penetration, patching, removal or fire stopping.

- Necessary ceiling tile or T-bar modifications, replacement and/or repair.

- Any and all millwork (moldings, trim, etc.) and/or furniture. All millwork and/or furniture or modifications to project millwork to accommodate the AV equipment is to be provided by others unless otherwise noted in this proposal.

- Painting, patching or finishing of architectural surfaces.

- Government/Owner furnished equipment (G/OFE) or equipment by others Installation of devices, systems, hardware, software as outlined in the statement of work.

- Permits (unless specifically provided for elsewhere in the contract).

- HVAC and plumbing relocation.

- Rough-in, bracing, framing, or finish trim carpentry for installation. Structural support unless otherwise specified

- Cutting, structural welding, or reinforcement of structural steel members required for support of assemblies, as required.
